1. General provisions

1.1. This Policy sets out how the personal data of visitors to the website https://vivlk.github.io/portfolio/ (the Site) is processed and protected. It has been prepared in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27 July 2006 “On Personal Data” (the Law) and is publicly available on the Site.

1.2. The personal data operator is Vladimir Igorevich Angelov, a professional income tax payer, TIN (INN) 253104049116 (the Operator). The Operator is personally responsible for organising the processing of personal data and for protecting it.

1.3. The Site uses cookies and the Yandex Metrica web analytics service only with the user's consent (clause 4.7). Until the user clicks “Allow” in the cookie notice, the Metrica tag is not loaded and no Metrica cookies are stored. Only technical flags are written to browser storage: that the intro screen has already been shown (sessionStorage) and which choice the user made in the cookie notice (localStorage). They contain no personal data.

1.4. The “Let's discuss your project” form on the Site does not send the answers to a server and does not store them. When the user clicks “Send”, the answers are turned into a draft message that opens in Telegram or in the user's email app; the user sends it themselves. The “Message on Telegram” and “Send an email” buttons work the same way.

2. What data is processed

2.1. Correspondence. The Operator processes only the information the user chooses to provide in an email or message:

  • the name or how the user would like to be addressed;
  • the email address or Telegram username the message was sent from, and the phone number if the user provided it or it is visible in their Telegram profile;
  • information about the project and other contents of the correspondence: the task, timeline and budget.

2.2. Visit statistics (only if the user has allowed cookies): Yandex Metrica cookie identifiers, IP address, information about the browser, device and operating system, URLs of the pages viewed and the referral source, and actions on the Site (following links, clicks, and submission of the brief form, without its contents). The Operator sees this information only as aggregated Metrica reports and does not match it with correspondence.

2.3. The Operator does not process special categories of personal data or biometric data and asks that such data not be included in correspondence. No decisions producing legal consequences are made on the basis of solely automated processing.

3. Purposes and legal grounds

3.1. Purpose: responding to the user's enquiry, discussing the project, preparing a proposal, and entering into and performing a contract for website design and development services.

3.2. Legal ground: the processing is necessary for entering into a contract at the user's initiative and for performing a contract to which the user is a party (Article 6(1)(5) of the Law).

3.3. Visit statistics are needed to understand which sections and projects people view and to improve the Site. The legal ground is the user's consent (Article 6(1)(1) of the Law), which the user gives by clicking “Allow” in the cookie notice and may withdraw at any time (clause 4.7).

4. How data is processed

4.1. Actions performed with data: receipt, recording, systematisation, storage, use, deletion and destruction. The processing method is mixed: with and without the use of automation tools.

4.2. Emails sent to angeloffvlad@yandex.ru are stored in the Operator's mailbox on the Yandex Mail service provided by YANDEX LLC (TIN (INN) 7736207543, 16 Lva Tolstogo St., Moscow, 119021), with servers in the Russian Federation.

4.3. Telegram messages are stored in Telegram, the messenger the user chose for communication, under that service's terms. The Operator does not copy correspondence from Telegram to other services.

4.4. Notifications. To avoid missing emails, the Operator receives a short notification in Telegram about a new email from the Site. The notification is generated by the Operator's service on the Yandex Cloud platform provided by Yandex.Cloud LLC (TIN (INN) 7704458262, OGRN 1187746678580, 16 Lva Tolstogo St., premises 528, Moscow, 119021), with servers in the Russian Federation. The service only checks whether there are unread emails and when they were received; it does not download or forward their contents. The notification does not contain the sender's name or address, or the text of the email.

4.5. Data is not transferred to other third parties or disseminated, except in cases expressly provided for by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

4.6. The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any hosting service, it receives the technical information that the browser sends when loading pages (IP address, browser data). The Operator has no access to this information and does not process it.

4.7. Cookies and Yandex Metrica. If the user clicks “Allow”, the Site loads the tag of Yandex Metrica, a service provided by YANDEX LLC (TIN (INN) 7736207543, 16 Lva Tolstogo St., Moscow, 119021). Metrica stores its own cookies in the browser (for example, _ym_uid, _ym_d) and sends Yandex the technical information about the visit listed in clause 2.2; Yandex processes it on the Operator's instructions under the service's terms of use (yandex.ru/legal/metrica_termsofuse). Recording of visitor actions (Webvisor) is not used. Consent can be withdrawn using the “Cookie settings” button at the bottom of any page of the Site → “Don't allow”: the tag stops loading and Metrica cookies are deleted from the browser. Declining cookies does not restrict access to the Site.

5. Processing periods and destruction

5.1. If no contract is concluded, correspondence is kept until the purpose of processing has been achieved, but for no longer than 12 months from the date of the last message. If a contract is concluded, correspondence is kept for the term of the contract and for the document retention period established by law.

5.2. At the user's request or once the purpose has been achieved, the Operator deletes the correspondence within 30 days (Article 21(4) and (5) of the Law), unless otherwise provided by law or the contract.

5.3. Consent to cookies remains valid until the user withdraws it. Metrica cookies are stored in the browser for no longer than one year; the Operator keeps aggregated Metrica reports for no longer than the period set by the service's terms.

6. Data protection

  • only the Operator has access to the mailbox and the Telegram account, and both are protected by two-factor authentication;
  • the notification service on Yandex Cloud has read-only access to the mailbox and keeps the app password in private settings;
  • data is transmitted only over secure protocols (HTTPS, IMAP over TLS);
  • if an incident results in the unlawful transfer of personal data, the Operator notifies Roskomnadzor (the Russian data protection authority) within 24 hours and reports the results of the investigation within 72 hours (Article 21(3.1) of the Law).

7. User rights

The user has the right to:

  • receive information about the processing of their personal data (Article 14 of the Law);
  • require their data to be rectified, blocked or destroyed if it is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or not necessary for the stated purpose;
  • withdraw consent to cookies and Yandex Metrica at any time using the “Cookie settings” button at the bottom of the page;
  • appeal against the Operator's actions to Roskomnadzor or in court.

Requests should be sent to angeloffvlad@yandex.ru. A request must include details that allow the Operator to find the correspondence and verify that it comes from the same person (for example, the address or username you wrote from and the approximate date of the message). The Operator responds within 10 working days; this period may be extended by no more than 5 working days, with the reasons stated (Articles 14 and 20 of the Law).

8. Final provisions

The Operator may amend this Policy. A new version takes effect upon publication on this page; the version date is shown at the top. Questions about this Policy can be sent to angeloffvlad@yandex.ru.
