process

How we work

Three stages, with payment after each. We approve the structure before I start designing.

  1. 01

    Brief and prototype

    We get on a call, go through the task and the audience, and agree on what counts as success: a call, an enquiry or a purchase. We approve the structure and the path to an enquiry before I start designing.

    sign-offPrototype and structure

  2. 02

    Design and development

    I design in your brand style and check it on a phone straight away. I build without unnecessary plugins so the site loads fast. Two rounds of revisions are included.

    sign-offMockup and build

  3. 03

    Launch and numbers

    I connect the domain and analytics, run test enquiries and write a guide for your team. A month later we get on a call, look at the numbers and decide what to improve.

    sign-offSite on your domain
