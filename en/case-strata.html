Stone catalogue · 2026

A website for a company that quarries natural stone and keeps it in its own “library” of slabs. Architects choose a specific slab by its number, and the site lets them do that online: examine the stone, plan the pattern and order samples.

The task

Natural stone is sold from photos, and then a slab with a different pattern turns up on site. Architects and designers need to see the exact slab they will get and understand how it will look on a wall or a worktop.

The site had to replace a trip to the warehouse: show the stone up close, explain the difference between finishes and let people put together a sample box without back-and-forth messaging.

The solution

The site opens with a descent into the quarry. The preloader counts the depth down to −120 m, and then the hero section shows the quarry and the library in figures: how many quarries and stone types there are and how many numbered slabs sit in the warehouse.

The library is a grid of eight stones with filters by stone type. Each stone lists its origin, thickness and remaining stock, and on hover a loupe magnifies the texture. Any stone can be added to the sample box or opened in the bookmatch studio.

Two tools help people choose a stone without a trip to the warehouse: the bookmatch studio and the wall try-on. The first shows how the veining will meet across adjacent slabs, the second shows the stone in a room. Both are captured in the recordings below.

Then comes the stone’s journey from quarry to installation in five steps, finished projects from Stockholm to Saint Petersburg and the sample box form: up to five stones, delivered within three working days.

What I did

  • Design and development of a one-page catalogue
  • Preloader as a descent into the quarry
  • Library of eight stones with filters and a texture loupe
  • Bookmatch studio: four layouts, cut line and angle
  • Four surface finishes and a wall try-on for the stone
  • The stone’s journey from quarry to installation in five steps
  • Sample box with a choice of up to five stones

In detail

  • 8 stones in the filterable library
  • 4 layouts in the bookmatch studio
  • 4 surface finishes for each stone
  • 5 samples per box, chosen right on the site
