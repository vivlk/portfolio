01 / 05
  1. 7 quarries · 12 benches in season

    Quarry

    We open a bench only where core samples show a seam worth cutting. Every block is numbered and photographed on six faces.

  2. blocks up to 28 t · graded a to d

    Block

    Each block is graded at the yard. Only one in four becomes library stone, the rest goes to tiles and aggregate.

  3. 72-wire saw · sequence kept

    Slab

    A diamond wire saw opens the block in one pass. Slabs stay in order, so a bookmatch is planned from real neighbours.

  4. 4 finishes · 1:1 slab scans

    Surface

    Polished, honed, leathered or bush-hammered, then sealed. Every slab is scanned at full size for the library.

  5. templating to ±1 mm

    Install

    Our fabricators template on site, cut the joints to the millimetre and set the slabs in the order you approved.
