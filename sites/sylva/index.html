    Fair questions

    Anything else — write to us. A farmer answers, not a bot.

    Store greens are cut 4–9 days before they reach the shelf and travel refrigerated. Ours are cut the morning of delivery and never see a cold truck — that’s the whole product.

    Pause any week from the app before Sunday 20:00. We simply don’t sow your share — nothing is wasted, nothing is charged.

    Anywhere within 40 minutes by bike from one of the six farms — that covers most of the city. Enter your address at checkout to check.

    No pesticides, no growth boosters — sealed indoor trays don’t need them. Scan any bunch’s QR and read the tray log yourself.

    Your box is built from whatever the harvest calendar shows for your delivery day. On top of that, add any crop as an extra bunch — or switch to Family and Chef for bigger splits.
